From omics to breakthrough

Hi, I am Jonas

I am a freelance computational biologist who helps biotech startups and pharma teams to make sense of complex genomic and transcriptomic datasets, so they can accelerate discovery, validate targets, and publish high-impact science with confidence.

With a PhD in Synthetic Biology, over 6 years of international computational consulting experience, and a track record of running robust pipelines for diverse multi-omics projects, I bring both wet-lab context and high-performance data engineering to your workflow.

Working with me is straightforward: I prioritize direct and frequent communication, deliver deeply customized solutions and treat your research goals as if they were my own.
